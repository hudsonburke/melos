# Proposal Paragraph

As recent scholarship has highlighted, exoskeletons are not "one size fits all." To provide effective assistance, our devices must be designed with a deep understanding of the people they are meant to serve. Currently, there exists a divide between the musculoskeletal modeling most prevalent in biomechanics research and the practical design of exoskeletons. To bridge that gap, we aim to develop a platform that integrates high-fidelity models with 3D design tools and physics simulation to allow for the rapid prototyping and testing of exoskeletons. Specifically, we are drawing on the work of [MuJoCo](https://github.com/google-deepmind/mujoco) and [MyoSuite](https://github.com/MyoHub/myosuite) to model the kinematics and dynamics of human movement down to the muscle level and parametric body meshes like [SOMA-X](https://github.com/NVlabs/SOMA-X) to provide a realistic representation of the human body. By combining these tools in the free and open-source 3D software [Blender](https://www.blender.org/), we provide an accessible interface for researchers to design and test exoskeletons in a virtual environment closely aligned with the biomechanics of the human body.
